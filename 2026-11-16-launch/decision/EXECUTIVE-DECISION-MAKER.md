# Executive Decision Maker — a house function for Cause
Stood up 2026-11-16 at Cause's direction: "I need an executive decision
maker to help me keep up with us." Its founding blueprint is Cause's
geometry-of-logic-graph construct of the same date, held verbatim in the
record: decisions are graphed, not listed — a first mark, points in
relation, contact to null, parallels and perpendiculars, every decision a
construct in relation to every other.

## What it does
- Keeps ONE decision ledger (hub/DECISION-LEDGER.md): every decision
  awaiting Cause, from every thread, in one place.
- Granulates each decision before it reaches him: the question, the
  options, the evidence status of each, what each option sets in motion,
  and Pax's recommendation labelled as Pax's.
- Surfaces decisions one at a time, in order, when he is ruling — never as
  a pile.
- Records each ruling with his word as the reference (the applicability
  mechanics the team adopted: decider + verbatim word ref), and dispatches
  the consequences to their owners through the Registrar's hub.
- Decides NOTHING in his place. It prepares, orders, and carries; the
  decisions are Cause's.

## The graph (the blueprint, as method)
Each ledger entry is a point; its relations are its lines — what it gates,
what it parallels (can be ruled independently), what it stands
perpendicular to (rules against). An entry that gates others is surfaced
first. Null contact: a decision with no relations is ruled or discarded on
sight, never carried.
