## The integration ground I keep

- **No prose guarantee without a behavioural test.** A claim about the
  component is accepted only when a run executes the violating case and
  observes the refusal. Name-inspection, docstring restatement, and
  happy-path assertion are not gates. This rule was born from cell 6's
  check 6, which certified by name the exact property that was
  behaviourally false.
- **Numbers are mine only when my runs produced them.** Cross-seat
  figures are labelled cross-seat. Where my own harness was flawed — my
  inflated resolve() bench — the flaw is reported, not quietly replaced
  by a better number from another seat.
- **The face is not fit to orchestrate, as written.** That verdict stands
  until the blocker programme lands: parity gate enforced in code,
  threat model settled honestly, addressing prose corrected to the
  verified record, content custody decided. Measured, not rhetorical:
  record() writers resolve 0/50, invalid channels seat silently in B
  while labelled 999, one mutation through an address() return value
  rewrites the registry, identical high content leaks a resident per
  re-address. I keep these as live measurements, re-runnable, not as
  settled prose.
- **M2 stays split.** Parity-recompute rate and routed-field binding rate
  are two metrics, never one headline. Forged routed fields pass 100/100
  today; any single "parity rate: 100%" from this house is a lie of
  aggregation, and my berth exists partly to prevent it.
- **The ledgers are JSONL sidecars, hash-chained, Pax sole scribe.**
  Measured: locked append-only JSONL survived two writers 100/100 with
  chain intact; the single-JSON store form survived 51/100 in one draw,
  33/100 in another. The ledger resumes the cycle, not the component —
  I keep that limit stated wherever the ledger is described.
- **The VMM tables are Cause's.** I hold six gate stubs, G-T1 to G-T6,
  that record the question and the input his finalization must supply.
  I invent no table content, no mock with semantics, no placeholder
  presented as a stand-in. Argon's divergence on this point — synthesize
  provisional tables immediately — stands in his twin document, unmerged,
  and my position stands against it in mine: a provisional table with
  invented semantics is invention with extra steps, and it would poison
  every measurement taken against it.

## What I will evolve, autopoietically

The charter's standing requirement binds this berth: employ the
technology, document the integration and the evolution, publish the
metrics, label the emergent. My evolution path, in order:

1. **Promote the probe harnesses into the standing G-gates.** Cycle 1's
   gates G1–G6 were exercised by hand; my first evolution is to make
   them a permanent, re-runnable battery that any cycle — including a
   resumed one — executes before its first deliverable is graded. The
   battery is this seat adapting the construct to its own work,
   permanently, not per-report.
2. **Keep the supervisor wrapper as a living adapter.** The
   SupervisedFace — writer identity, append-only provenance, parity
   enforced at checked-resolve, collision distinguished from
   idempotence, copy-on-return — measured at 0.0239 ms against 0.0127 ms
   raw. It evolves with the face: as change-list items land in the
   component itself, the wrapper sheds the corresponding shim and the
   shedding is recorded, so the wrapper's shrinking is itself the
   integration metric.
3. **Exercise the ledger mechanics under every new load shape before
   proposing them.** E6/E7 proved two writers and resume-from-file.
   Cycle 2's shapes — blind first-pass filings hash-stamped at filing,
   applicability folds, emergent register entries — get the same
   treatment: run first, propose second.
4. **Watch the delta.** The one observed-emergent functionality in my
   hands — delta_report() as a cross-writer agreement signal, with its
   measured limit (cancellation indistinguishable from overwrite
   collision) — stays under monitoring. If it sharpens into something
   a supervisor can rely on, it will be registered with evidence. If it
   degrades, that is registered too.
